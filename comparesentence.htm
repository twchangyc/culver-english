好壞英文句子之比較與修改

●代表錯誤的或不良的句子；包括語法錯誤、語意不明、句子統合有誤、句意重點含糊等。

○代表修正後，優良正確的句子。☆代表最優良正確的句子。

1 ●I only shot one deer. ○I shot only one deer.
2 ●I sat watching the tide come in on the front porch.○I sat on the front porch watching the tide.
3 ●He neither came by train nor by boat.○He came neither by train nor by boat.
4 ●He came forward as I entered the room and held out his hand to greet me.○As I entered the room he came forward and held out his hand to greet me.
5 ●On approaching the university a large ornamental gate is passed through.○On approaching the university we pass through a large ornamental gate.
6 ●Having been properly boiled we are perfectly safe in drinking this water.○Having been properly boiled this water is perfectly safe to drink.☆As this water has been properly boiled it is safe to drink.
7 ●Chang told his father that his coat was too old-fashioned.○Chang told his father that his father's coat was too old-fashioned.☆Chang told his father, "Your coat is too old-fashioned."
8 ●October 10 is a national holiday in Taiwan. They always celebrate the day very enthusiastically.○October 10 is a national holiday in Taiwan. The Chinese in Taiwan celebrate the day very enthusiastically.☆The people in Taiwan always observe October 10 very enthusiastically as a national holiday.
9 ●My friend and brother came to see me.○My friend and my brother came to see me.
10 ●She married him not because she loved him but he had money.○She married him not because she loved him but because he had money.
11 ●He lay for some minutes without consciousness and almost breathing.○He lay for some minutes without consciousness and almost without breathing.
12 ●I always have and always will take an interest in photography.○I always have taken and always will take an interest in photography.
13 ●They were all exhausted and one of them almost dead.○They were all exhausted and one of them was almost dead.
14 ●In the fall he returned to college and all his energies were now devoted to his studies.○In the fall he returned to college and now devoted to his studies.
15 ●The boat drifted to the shore and we landed on the island where the people received us with the utmost kindness.○We drifted to the shore and landed on the island where we were received by the people with the utmost kindness.
16 ●I got that necklace in Paris at one of those cunning little shops on the Rue Rivoli where I met the Smiths, and we had lunch together in an open-air cafe.○I got that necklace in Paris at one of those cunning little shops on the Rue Rivoli. I met the Smiths there and we had lunch together in an open-air cafe.
17 ●My brother went to college and I remained on the farm.○My brother went to college but I remained on the farm.
18 ●It was a cool afternoon and we had a fast game of tennis.○Since it was a cool afternoon  we had a fast game of tennis.
19 ●Her mother had not heard that she was ill, I think.○I think that her mother had not heard that she was ill.☆Her mother, I think,  had not heard that she was ill
20 ●He turned away, almost speechless with grief, when he saw the wreck.○When he saw the wreck he turned away, almost speechless with grief.
21 ●Broken bones, violent deaths, falls and bruises were of daily occurrence.○Falls, bruises, broken bones, and bruises were of daily occurrence.
22 ●I saw a most wonderful sight when I was on my way to school this morning.○When I was on my way to school this morning, I saw a most wonderful sight.
23 ●I was walking down the street this morning when I witnessed a horrible accident.○When I was walking down the street this morning, I witnessed a horrible accident.
24 ●A cow kicked over a lamp, which accident caused the great Chicago fire.○When a cow kicked over a lamp,  the great Chicago fire was caused.
25 ●I saw him climbing the fence with his knife between his teeth; I was determined to get it from him.○---, I was determined to get that knife from him.
26 ●He said, "I shall never see her again."○He said, "I shall never see her again. Never!"
27 ●He ordered the captain to order the soldiers to preserve good order.○He directed the captain to see that the soldiers preserved good order.
28 ●He laughed at the observation that arbitration might succeed between nation and nation.○He laughed at the idea that nations would arbitrate their differences.
29 ●She was in anxious of marrying him.○She was anxious to marry him.
30 ●He is no good. Why, because he has no money.○He is of no use because he has no money.○He is not wanted because he has no money.
31 ●This essay does not inform any facts to us.○This essay does not inform us of any facts.○This essay does not give us any information.
32 ●They looked at and took a conversation with each other.○They looked at each other and held a conversation together.☆They looked at each other and conversed together.
33 ●Jim told Tom that he had got marry with Mary.○Jim told Tom that he and Mary had married( or, gotten married.)
34 ●Jim won a prize a year after since he read the announcement.○Jim won a prize a year after he read the announcement.
35 ●It never minds for us to write these sentences.○It is not necessary for us to write these sentences.○It is not important for us to write these sentences.
36 ●These notes make us much easier to remember.○These notes make it easier for us to remember.
37 ●They were impossible to do this. ○They were unable to do this. ☆It was impossible for them to do this.
38 ●She was so pretty that he had never seen before.○She was a prettier girl than he had ever seen before.☆He had never before seen such a pretty girl as she was.
39 ●Because of the work is very simple they can do it easily.○Because the work is very simple they can do it easily.
40 ●This makes them lacking of effort.○This makes them lack effort.
41 ●He placed every house with one of the flowers of hope from his basket.○He placed one of the flowers of hope from his basket in every house.
42 ●My tears are full in my eyes.○My eyes are full of tears.
43 ●He was not aware of that most of the people are never satisfied even they had their hopes obtained.○He was not aware that most people are never satisfied even though they obtain their hopes.( or, have obtained)
44 ●Man can succeed everything by his diligent working.○Man can succeed in everything by working diligently.☆Man can succeed in everything by diligent work.
45 ●We should respect all women no matter they are young or old.○We should respect all women no matter if they are young or old. ☆We should respect all women whether they are young or old.
46 ●Unemployment causes them impossible to get their living.○Unemployment makes it impossible for them to get a living.
47 ●The railways are not repaired nor exchanged with new materials.○The railways are not repaired nor are new  materials used in exchange for old materials that are worn out. ☆The railways are not repaired nor are the old worn out materials replaced by new ones.☆They do not repair the railways nor replace the worn out parts with new materials.
48 ●They worked hard day after night.○They worked hard day after day.( or, day and night)
49 ●No sooner after their dinner, Helen began to use her magic wand.○No sooner had they eaten their dinner than Helen began to use her wand.
50 ●They went to an island which they know not of.○They went to an island which they did not know.
51 ●A man whose conscience tells him that he has done no wrong should not be afraid of slander.○A man should not be afraid of slander if his conscience tells him that he has done no wrong.☆If a man's conscience tells him that he has done no wrong, he should not be afraid of slander.
52 ●He fired without realizing the consequences of what he was doing the shot that was heard around the world.○Without realizing the consequences of what he was doing, he fired the shot that was heard around the world.
53 ●I am sure that she would, unless she was prevented, answer my letter.○I am sure she would answer my letter, unless she was prevented.
54 ●I wanted to particularly know what color of pencil to use.○I wanted to know particularly(, or particularly to know) what color of pencil to use.
55 ●On Sunday afternoon I often like to ride on bicycle to go to the place where I have not visited before.○On Sunday afternoon I  like to ride on a bicycle to places which I have not visited before.☆Sunday afternoons I  like to ride a bicycle to places which I have not visited before.
56 ●Our school has designed to give on every Saturday night, a social meeting in which all the students and teachers will enjoy together.○Our school has planned to give every Saturday night a social meeting in which all the students and teachers will enjoy themselves together.☆Every Saturday night our school gives a social meeting for students and teachers to enjoy themselves together.
57 ●Last Saturday all the students crowded in the China Daily News for it said that the famous Chinese movie star committed suicide herself, some of the girls after all wept with sympathy.○Last Saturday all the students crowded around the China Daily News, for it said that a famous Chinese movie star had committed suicide, and some of the girls wept with sympathy.( or, for grief)
58 ●We have beautiful library, big laboratory, and due to the reason that it is far away from the center of Hualien, bad habits which prevail in the city have no much affection upon us.○We have a beautiful library and a big laboratory; and because it is far away from Hualien, bad habits which prevail in the city have not much effect upon us.☆We have a beautiful library and a big laboratory; and we are far away from Hualien we are not subject to the bad influences of the city.
59 ●The man who substituted you in that game had no team work with us, however, they had promised to play with us later, as soon as your health is recovered, we shall do our best to win the game back.○The man who substituted for you in that game had no team work with us. However, they have promised to play with us later, as soon as your health is restored, and then we shall do our best to win the game back.
60 ●I had come to the school nearly six weeks.○I came to this school nearly six weeks ago.☆I have been attending this school nearly six weeks.
61 ●The scenes of the school are very attractive. We  can hear the beautiful songs from the chapel, and we can see many sportsmen playing football game in the playground.○Life at our school has attractions of various kinds; we hear beautiful songs in the chapel, and we see many students playing football on the playground.☆Life at our school has attractions of various kinds; in the chapel we hear beautiful songs, while outside on the playground students play football and other games.
62 ●In the afternoon, all the members of our Christian fellowship went to visit a park near our school and the center of the city. The building are very fine. I hope you will visit there some day.○In the afternoon all the members of our Christian fellowship group visited a park and the civic center near our school. It is a beautiful place with some fine buildings, which I hope you will visit some day.☆
63 ●When the examiner (in the tramcar) inquired my ticket than I gave him my ticket, but he said that is not the right ticket.○When the examiner asked for my ticket I gave it to him, but he said that it was not the right ticket.☆
64 ●You had taught me how to play baseball, but I don't play baseball now, because there are not much girls to play baseball.○You taught me how to play baseball, but I don't play it now, for there are not many girls to play it.☆I learned to play baseball from you, but I have no chance to play it now as we have not enough girls here to play it.
65 ●His family has one father, three old brothers and one young sister.○The family consists of a father, three grown up sons and one little girl.☆
66 ●I like to make friend with him, but his family is so bad. Such friend may I make? Mother, will you answer my question?○I should like to make friends with him but his family is very bad. Will you please tell me, mother, if I may make such a friendship?☆May I have such a friend? Mother, will you please answer my question.
67 ●When spring comes, the trees and grasses are turned into green and the birds are singing on the branches of the trees. I feel that I am younger and life is worth.○When spring comes the grass and trees turn green and  birds sing in the branches. It is good to be young and life seems worth while.☆When spring comes and the grass and trees turn green, with birds singing in the branches, it is good to be young and life seems worth while.
68 ●I spent most time in library, when the class was none. Sometimes after finished the work, I may take a walk along the campus.○I spend the most of my time in the library when I do not have a class; but after I have finished my work, I sometimes take a walk around the campus.
69 ●We had a social party in the past two Saturday nights. There were famous singers sang songs, and we had enjoyed ourselves very much indeed.○We had social parties the past two Saturday nights, when famous singers sang for us and we enjoyed ourselves very much indeed.☆We had social parties the past two Saturday nights, when we heard famous singers and enjoyed ourselves very much indeed.
70 ●Greatly to my sadness, I have not met you again since my graduation. But, your advice is still remaining a deep impression in my heart.○Much to my grief I have not met you at all since my graduation, but your advice  still makes a deep impression on my heart.☆----still remains in my heart.
71 ●They led me directly to their parlor. It is a small, clean, beautiful and very much artistic room. Many pictures were hanged around the room.○They led me directly to their parlor which was a small clean room, artistically decorated with many beautiful pictures on the walls.
72 ●Last Sunday as the whether was so fine. Marry and I went to Hualien which is a marvelous place that I had never seen before.○Last Sunday, as the weather was very fine, Mary and I went to Hualien, which is a marvelous place such as I had never seen before.☆----, which is more marvelous than any place that I had ever seen.
73 ●I can sing few songs and I hope you visiting my school and hearing our music.○I can sing few songs and I hope that you will visit our school and hear our music.
74 ●Since I am very weak in English I wish I can study hard and be improved some days.○Since I am very weak in English I wish to study hard and improve some day.
75 ●Here I am at Taipei for the first time. I arrived Taipei yesterday and I was right my way to visit Mr. Lee at once.○Here I am at Taipei for the first time. I arrived yesterday and went to visit Mr. Lee at once.
