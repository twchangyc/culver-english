第 十 個 基 本 句 型

請各位英文教師，善用以下的題例，先Copy下來，再依學生程度不同，予以增減內容，以期達到教學目標

主 語 述 語
ＮＰ Ｖｔ－ｃ NP1(受 詞) NP2 or Adj(受 詞 補 語)
They call her Mary.
We think it important to be honest.
註解1

Vt~c「consider 類的及物動詞」：consider, think, call, find, leave, make, keep, believe, leave, imagine, judge, prove, label等。
註解2

有些動詞後面，只有「形容詞」可以當受詞補語。這類動詞是：get, feel, set, wear, like, prefer, want, paint等。例句：I like my tea hot.//She wears her hair long.//We set him free.
註解3

同一個動詞，常常可以出現在不同的句型裡。看詳解。

　

例句：

1.John called you a fool.→比較 John called you a taxi.【第八句型】

2.We considered him a fool. （or） We considered him foolish.

3.They thought it a wonderful idea. （or） They thought the idea wonderful.

4.I think her (to be) a loving girl. 我認為她是個可愛的女孩。

5.I found him a sick man.

6.Jane wears her hair short.

7.I found the hotel delightful.

8.I painted the walls white.

9.I want my coffee strong.

10.Bob plays his radio loud.

11.Do you find English simple or complicated?

12.I like my steak medium.

13.I prefer my vegetables cooked.

14. John keeps everything clean.

15. We have made our shoes muddy.

同一個動詞，常常可以出現在不同的句型裡

(1)appear: Bill appeared surprised.（第五句型） // Bill appeared suddenly.（第六句型）

(2)call: They called me a taxi.（第八句型） // They called me a fool.（第十句型）

(3)think: I think he will succeed.（第七句型） // I think him a fool.（第十句型）
