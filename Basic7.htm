主 語

述 語
ＮＰ

Ｖｔ┼ＮＰ(Adv -m)（及物動詞┼名詞組）
I respect her.（我尊敬她。）
註解1

Vt(transitive verb)，這類名詞組在語法上叫做及物動詞的賓語(object)。賓語後面可以用狀態副詞來修飾。
註解2

有些及物動詞後面還可以跟著「介副詞」。這個時候，介副詞可以出現在賓語的前面，也可以出現在賓語的後面。也有人稱這類「動詞+介副詞」為：（可分的）雙字動詞。請參考本頁底的說明。
註解3 延伸句型1：Ｓ＋Ｖｔ＋〔ｔｏ＋Ｖ〕（以不定詞為受詞）
註解4

延伸句型2：Ｓ＋Ｖt＋(pro)noun ＋ 〔ｔｏ＋Ｖ〕
註解5

延伸句型3：Ｓ＋Ｖｔ＋there to be＋noun, etc.
註解6

延伸句型4：Ｓ＋Ｖｔ＋（pro）noun＋Adv.
註解7 不能用狀態副詞修飾的動詞。

例句：

1.John speaks English well.

2.We finished the game (quickly).

3.The boy opened the door (loudly).

4.The girl is riding her bicycle (slowly).

5.David turned on the radio.=David turned the radio on.=David turned it on.（可分動詞）

6.Jane is looking over her exercise.=Jane is looking her exercise over.（可分動詞）

7.I must look up the words in the dictionary.

=I must look the words up in the dictionary.

=I must look them up in the dictionary.（5, 6, 7,中的turn on, look over, 和 look up 乃可分動詞）

* 8.Jim looked at his friends.（不可分動詞）

* 9.The dog is waiting for his master.

（#8, #9 中的 look at 和 wait for 乃不可分動詞）

註解：不可分雙字動詞的形成，乃是它是由「動詞+介詞」構成，所以又稱為「介詞動詞」(prepositional verbs)。它與「可分動詞」最大的區別是，它的賓語一定要放在介詞的後面，不能放在前面。試比較：John looked at his picture.(O)，不可寫成John looked his picture at.(×)

可分雙字動詞 〔 動詞 ＋ 副詞 〕：

1.put on 2.take off 3.pick up 4.put down 5.give away 6.send out 7.knock down 8.lock up 9.bring in 10.take out 11.get out 12.wind up 13.laugh down 14.pack up 15.back up 16.clean out 17.throw away 18.bring about

例句：

1.He took his hat off.

2.I had my tooth out.

3.He put on his hat. = He put it on.

4.The car knocked her down.

5.She gave away every penny she had.

6.He has brought about her ruin.

注意1：如果動詞之後的賓語是「人稱代名詞」(me, you, him, her, it, them, etc)，或「不定代名詞」(someone, something, anyone, anything, everyone, everything, etc)，那麼賓語一定放在副詞之前。試比較：Bill turned it on.(O)，不可寫成Bill turned on it.(×)

注意2：如果賓語是名詞組的話，則副詞可放在「名詞組」之前或之後，試比較：Sue turned on the radio.(O)=Sue turned the radio on.(O)
