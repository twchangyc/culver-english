Some Tips of Letter Writing

◎Write with a Design（先有腹稿）

Good writing comes only from clear thinking. Before you writing a letter, decide upon the central idea of your message, make a mental inventory of all the things you want to say in the message, and then arrange these items in a logical and effective order for presentation. Give prominence to the more important items by making them the topic sentences of separate paragraphs and relegate（降級） the lesser important ones to the supporting or subordinate positions.

A basic structural design is essential to every kind of writing. Writing without a design is like sailing without a direction. Of course, best writing sometimes springs from impulse and emotion and assumes the resemblance of an outpouring in disarray, yet on close examination, one could always find in it a secret plan which gives unity and coherence（一貫性） to the language and helps make it articulate（清晰的） and sensible. Only a demented（瘋的） person will say things without going through the mental process of thinking and deliberation, and what he says is nothing but nonsense.

Write your letter on the basis of a suitable design in simple and natural speech, and then revise it in accordance with good usage. The adequate vocabulary and the feeling for good usage and idiom-which make the difference in the quality of writing-can be acquired only by wide intelligent reading. And in no other way whatever.

◎Place Emphasis at the Beginning and the End（信的開頭與結尾，是訊息重點）

The beginning and the end are the two emphatic positions in letter. The beginning is emphatic because it attracts the initial attention of the reader, but the end is emphatic because it makes the last impression.

Do not clutter your first paragraph with details concerning the receiving of the letter, the date, the contents, and so on. Begin your letter with a vigorous personal message that will draw the attention of the reader and induce him to read through. True, in writing a reply, it is often necessary to make reference to the previous correspondence in order to make it easier for the reader to follow what you are talking about. These mechanical data, however, can very well be brought in after you have presented the more important message.

The last paragraph should be the place for a "clincher"- a command or a request that will urge the reader to do what you wish him to do. Do not close your letter with a participial phrase, for it has the double weakness of being an incomplete grammatical construction and a hackneyed expression. Keep the ending for a clear-cut, effective statement, not a worn-out, insignificant phrase.（不可用「分詞片語」做結尾）

◎Be Clear and Concise（表達要明確簡潔）

Verboseness does not contribute to clarity. On the contrary, it obscures the message. Vigorous writing is concise. A sentence should contain no unnecessary words, a paragraph no unnecessary sentences, just as a drawing should have no unnecessary lines and a machine no unnecessary parts.

Avoid stringy compound sentences or a series of short simple sentences. Make effective use of subordination to give grammatical focus to the main clauses. Attempt at all times to express a maximum of thought in a minimum of words.（用最少字表達最多想法）

◎Use Definite, Specific, Concrete Language（使用實在具體語言）

Avoid abstract and general terms, for they give only vague ideas. Use specific, concrete words that will fit the exact shade of your meaning and can create a picture in your reader's mind.

The test for specific, concrete language is whether or not it can answer one or more of these questions: Exactly who? Exactly what? Precisely when? Exactly where? Precisely how?

Nouns and verbs are the bones and flesh of a sentence. The more concrete nouns and active verbs you use, the more forceful your writing. Piling up adjectives and adverbs does not add definiteness or intensity. When the modifiers are used unskillfully, they only weaken a statement, making it vague, subjective, and unconvincing. The better technique is to use specific nouns and verbs that can convey the intended meaning without the help of the modifiers.

Another way to help make your language concrete and convincing is to provide solid factual information. If you want to describe the nutritional value of a food product, supply a detailed analysis of the food contents of the product. If you want to describe a curvaceous girl, state her measurements.

◎Give Character to Your Letter（顯出你的風格）

A letter that has character reveals the individuality and the distinctiveness of the writer's personality. It should be free from worn-out and mechanical expressions. Be original in your thinking and writing. You cannot hope to develop character in your letters if you merely copy or imitate the so-called model letters in a textbook.

◎Observe the Work Rules（沈靜一陣子，再修正）

Don't stretch yourself too far. Three hours is about the limit of concentrated effort in writing. After that, you are likely to begin writing involved sentences and repetitive phrases. Take a break when you reach this point.

After you have completed a letter, lay it aside for some time and then come back to it with a fresh eye. You will see more clearly the flaws in the writing. Don't hesitate to revise and rewrite. Few writers are so expert that they can produce a piece of good writing on the first try.

Keep on your desk a good dictionary, a thesaurus（辭典）, and an authoritative handbook on writing. And use them!
