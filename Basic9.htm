第 九 個 基 本 句 型

請各位英文教師，善用以下的題例，先Copy下來，再依學生程度不同，予以增減內容，以期達到教學目標

主 語 述 語
ＮＰ Ｖｔ－ｅ NP1(受 詞) NP2(受 詞 補 語)
The city elected Mr. Brown mayor.
註解1

Vt~e(elect類的及物動詞，transitive verbs of elect-type)，如：elect, choose, call, vote, appoint, name, nominate. designate, crown, christen等。
註解2

第九基本句型與第八基本句型，在述語部份都有二個名詞組，但其功用不同。第八的二個名詞組，是指兩個不同的人或物。第九的二個名詞組，是指同一個人或物。見比較。

屬於本句型的動詞：keep, leave, find, make, render, elect, appoint, call, name, think, consider, believe, suppose, feel, know, judge, report, declare, paint, dye, boil, cut, push, throw, designate, crown, christen.

例句：

1.We call him an idiot（白痴）.

2.They elected him President.

3.They will name the ship Queen Mary.

4.Bill named his new boat "Heaven".

5.They named the baby Christine.

6.I named the dog "Snow White".

7.The committee appointed him chairman.

8.They appointed him manager.

9.The company appointed him superintendent.

10.The students chose him president.

11.Did you choose Bill spokesman for the family?

12.They voted Jane the most popular girl.

13.They nominated him treasure.

14.I found it her.

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

15.We regard him as a danger to society.

16.They chose Mr. Green as their leader.

17.She described him as really clever.

18.They took it as read.他們把它視為已宣讀通過（不必再討論了）。

（15~18句，第一受詞NP1後「+as+補語(noun or adj)」，此類動詞：accept, acknowledge, characterize, choose, describe, know, interpret, rank, recognize, regard, represent, take, treat, view, reject等。）

注意：用regard時，as不可省略。

例句：I regard the situation as serious.我認為事態嚴重。

     They regarded the work as having been done.他們認為那件工作已完成。

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

19.She heard her name called.（19~20句，受詞+p.p.）

20.I will have my watch repaired.

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

21.Let them come in.

22.I will have you believe that.

23.I saw the boy cross the street.

24.She felt her heart beat wildly.

（21~24句，V+受詞+V原形，此類動詞乃「使役動詞和感官動詞」：make, let, bid, have, help, // see, hear, feel, find, know, behold, watch, observe, perceive, notice.）

比較第八基本句型與第九基本句型的二個名詞組

They gave John their captain ( as a partner ).（第八句型，John和captain不是同一人；他們把他們的隊長送給約翰，當作夥伴。）

They elected John their captain.（第九句型，John 和captain指同一人；他們選舉約翰為他們的隊長。）
