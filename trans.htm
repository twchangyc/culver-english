（1）

[How often] [How long] [How far] 等的翻譯

1.你一星期洗幾次澡？How often a week do you take a bath?
2.我每隔一天洗一次澡。I take a bath every other day.
3.你刷幾次牙？一天刷二次。How often ( How many times ) do you clean your teeth? Twice a day.
4.你在紐約的朋友家裡住了幾天？How long did you stay with your friend in New York?
5.住了一星期，從十日到十六日為止。For a week, from the 10th to the 16th.
6.你在台北停留幾天？How long ( How many days ) did you stay in Taipei?
7.你在車站等了幾小時？How long ( How many hours ) did you wait at the station?
8.這本書我能借到什麼時候？How long may I keep this book?
9.我什麼時候該歸還這本書呢？When shall I return this book?
10.從你家到學校有多遠？How far is it from your house to the school?
11.讓我想想看，大約有一哩半。Well, let me see, it is about a mile and a half.
12.從這裡到花蓮有多遠？How far is it from here to Hualien?
13.你早上走了多遠？How far did you go this morning?
14.你一天能走幾哩？How far ( How many miles ) can you walk in a day?
15.你爸爸一星期洗幾次澡呢？他每天洗。How often does your father take a bath in a week? He takes it every day.
16.你到過比爾家幾次了？我去過二次。How often did you go to Bill’s house. I went there twice.
17.你一小時能跑多遠？我一小時能跑三哩半。How far can you run in an hour? I can run five miles and a half in an hour.
18.今天下午你走了多遠？我走到台北車站那麼遠。How far did you go this afternoon?  I walked as far as Taipei station.
19.這本書我能借多久呢？借到下星期五。How long may I keep this book? You may keep it till next Friday.
20.他在台中的朋友家裡待了多久？他待了十天左右。How long did he stay with his friend in Taichung? He stayed there for about ten days.
21.他每三天去看醫生。He goes to the doctor's  every three days.
22.從這裡到鄰村有多遠？嗯，大約三哩半。How far is it from here to the next village? Well, it is about three miles and a half.

（2）

否定句的翻譯

1.不要說謊。Never tell a lie. ( or ) Don't tell a lie.
2.他從不守約。He never keep his word. ( or ) He does not keep his word.
3.他不應得到成功。He does not deserve it.
4.懶惰的人做什麼事都不會成功。An idle man never succeeds in anything.
5.歲月不待人。Time and tide wait for no man.
6.我在途中沒遇見任何人。I met nobody on the way. ( or ) I did not meet anybody on the way.
7.這男孩沒向我要求任何東西。The boy asked me for nothing. ( or ) The boy did not ask me for anything.
8.他雖然努力用功，但是功課一點也沒進步。Though he works hard, he makes no progress in his studies.
9.我在這幅畫裡可以看見二個男孩，但是沒有女孩。I can see two boys in the picture, but no girls.
10.沒有任何事令他高興。Nothing pleased him.
11.沒有空氣的話，沒有動物能活。Without air no animal can live.
12.這個問題對他們來說太困難了。It was too difficult for them.
13.學生當中沒有一個人能正確地回答這個問題。None of the students could answer the question.
14.這些書當中沒有一本值得讀。None of these books are worth reading.
15.我的朋友當中沒有一人出席。None of my friends were present.
16.人不努力，任何事都不能成功。Nobody can succeed in anything without effort.
17.懶惰的學生學業絕不會進步。An idle student makes no progress in his studies.
18.郵差一天送信三次，可是昨天沒有帶信來。A postman delivers mail three times a day. But he brought no letter yesterday.
19.魚住在水中，沒有水的話，他們活不下去。Fish live in water. None of them can live without water.
20.太陽絕不會從西邊昇上來。The sun never rises in the west.

（3）

名詞組[Verb+er, Verb+ing] 及形容詞組[Noun+y, Verb+ing]的翻譯

1.他從早到晚努力工作，他是個勤勞的人。He works hard from morning till night. He is a hard worker.
2.星星在夜間閃爍，但是多雲的夜晚我們看不見星星。Stars shine at night, but on a cloudy night we can see none of them.
3.我喜歡游泳，但是游得不好。I am fond of swimming, but I cannot swim well.
4.他是個快跑健將，他的同學沒有人能像他跑得那麼快。He is a fast runner and none of his classmates can run so fast as he.
5.颱風的日子你不能去游泳，因為浪高而危險。On a windy day you must not go swimming, for the waves are high and dangerous.
6.絕不要說謊，沒有人會相信說謊者。Never tell a lie. No one will trust a liar.
7.我喜歡徒步旅行勝於搭火車旅行。I like traveling on foot better than traveling by train.
8.今天早上我想睡覺，因為昨晚沒睡好。I am sleepy this morning, for I did not sleep well last night.
9.去年我第一次嘗試滑雪旅行。I tried traveling on skies for the first time last year.
10.那是一個非常有趣的旅行方法。It is a most interesting method of traveling.

（4）

連接詞與介詞

1.  星期三下午放學後，一年級學生參觀了國立圖書館。The first year boys paid a visit to the National Library after school on Wednesday afternoon. 
2.  星期一早上早餐之後，我到台北圖書館。On Monday morning after the breakfast I went to the Taipei library. 
3.  這項工作三月開始而十月便完成了。The work was started in March and it was finished before October. 
4.  「為什麼他延期直到星期六才離去?」「因為他的父親突然生病了。」“Why did he put off his departure till Saturday?” “Because his father suddenly became ill.”
5. 「你咋晚睡覺前沒關燈嗎?」「是的，我沒有(關燈)。」 Didn’t you turn off the light before you go to bed last night? No. I didn’t (turn it off). 
6.  我一直保管著他的行李，直到他來取回。I kept the luggage for him till he called for it. 
7. 「你們在天黑之阭便越過了山嗎?」「是的，而且我們抵達旅館時天還亮著。」 “Did you cross the mountain before dark?” “Yes, we did, and when we got to the inn it was still light. 
8.我沒能碰見他，因為他在我抵達台北之前離開了台北。  I could not see him, because he left Taipei before I reached there.  
9.天黑後，他才到家。He got home after it was dark.
10.他在我吃早餐之前，便出發了。He set out before I finished my breakfast.

現在單純式

1.他很守時，從不爽約。 He is punctual. He never breaks his words. 
2.我早上六點起床，七點吃早飯，七點四十五分上學去。 I get up at six in the morning, take breakfast at seven and go to school at a quarter to eight.
3. 雨正下很大。我們等到雨停吧!It is raining heavily. Let us wait till it stops.
4. 你怎麼啦？你看來臉色蒼白。What is the matter with you? You look pale. 
5.我頭痛，而且感覺非常冷。 I have a headache and I feel very cold.
6.我要到車站去接爸爸。爸爸早上從台南回來。 I am going to the station to see my father. Father is coming back from Tainan this morning. 
7."考試何時開始？下週開始。"When does the examination begin?" "It begins next week.
8.我離開台北之前必須打個電報給父親。 I must send a wire to my father before I leave Taipei.
9.我總是用冷水洗臉。I always wash my face in cold water.
10.地球一年繞太陽一次。The earth moves round the sun once in a year.

【Shall】 【Will】

1.  天很陰暗，今晚會下雨。The sky is very cloudy. We shall have rain this evening. 
2.  下個星六我們大概會有英文考試。We shall probably have an examination in English next Saturday. 
3. 他要是不用功，考試將會不及格。 He will fail in the examination if he does not work. 
4.  他上課總是遲到。今天又會遲到。He always comes to school late. He will be late again this morning.  
5.  在一、二週內後將有櫻花盛開，因為我們每年總是在那個時期左右有櫻花。We shall have cherry blossoms in a week or two, for we always have them about that time of the year. 
6.  「假如你搭巴士去，什麼時候抵達那裏?」「後於中午之前抵達。」“What time shall you get there if you go by bus?” “I shall get there before noon.“
7.  開學典禮將在四月十一日舉行。後那一天沒課。The opening ceremony will be held on the l lth of April (or on April l l.) we shall have no lessons that day. 
8.雨季在六月初開始。今年大約那個時候後也將有很多的雨量。 The rainy season sets in at the beginning of June. We shall have much rain about that time of this year, too. 
9.假如你不用功，你將會失敗。If you don't work hard, you will fail.
10.有一天他自然會後悔的。Some day he will certainly be sorry for it. 

（7）

【Shall】 【Will】第二部分

1. 「明天早上你要我幾點叫你起來呢」「請在五點以前叫我。」 “What time shall I call you tomorrow morning?” “Please call me before five.”
2. 「他回來時，你要他幫你把這封信投郵嗎？」「請叫他這樣做。」 “Shall he post this letter for you when he comes back?” “Please let him do so.”
3.「請你告訴我往博物館去的路好嗎？」「好的。我很樂意。」  “Will you direct me to the museum?” “Yes, certainly I will.”
4. 「假如你今晚不忙，請在七點半來如我共進晚餐好嗎？」「謝謝，我很樂意來。」 “If you are not busy this evening, won’t you come and have dinner with me at half past seven?” “Thank you, I will come with pleasure.”
5. 你不渴嗎？要我幫你泡茶嗎？ Are you not thirsty? Shall I make tea for you?
6.  你們到車站了！我買車票時，請幫我看著行李好嗎？Here we are at the station. Will you please look after my luggage while I buy tickets?
7.  要我幫你把手錶送到錶店嗎？大發條已斷了。Shall I take you watch to the watchmaker’s? The mainspring is broken.
8. 「你要我怎樣處理這書的包裏呢？」「請把它放在我的書桌上。」 “What shall I do with this book package?” “Please put it on my desk.”
9.「要我幫你寄這封信嗎？」「是的，拜託」"Shall I post the letter for you?" "Yes, please." 
10.再來一杯茶怎麼樣？Will you (Won't you) have another cup of tea?

（8）

[Must] [May] [Can]

1.這不是我的帽子，一定是別人的。This is not my hat. It must be somebody else’s.
2.你必須馬上把它告訴你父親。You must inform your father of it at once.
3.你最好帶著傘因為可能隨時會下雨You had better take your umbrella with you, as it may rain any moment.
4.老師，默寫必須每隔一行寫嗎？Sir, must we write the diction every other line?
5.多麼大而漂亮的花兒啊！它們一定是玫瑰花。What big and pretty flowers they are! They must be roses.
6.我看不懂這本書，請借我一本較簡單的。I cannot understand this book. Please lend me an easier one.
7.我查了字典，但仍不了解這段話的意思。I consulted my dictionary, but I could not understand the meaning of this passage.
8.他不可能是個誠實的人，沒有人相信他的話。He cannot be an honest man. Nobody trusts his words. (or, trusts him.)
9.如果你喜歡，你可以把這本書帶回家。You may take this book home, if you like.
10.地板再也不能承受任何重量了。The floor could not bear the weight any longer.

（9）

[Have to~] [Be able to ~]

1.因為時間還很充裕，他將能夠搭二點半的火車。As there is plenty of time; he will be able to catch the 2:30 train.
2.要是錯過這班火車，我們將無法在下午五點以前抵達台南。If we miss the train, we shall not be able to reach Tainan before 5 p. m.
3.假如我們不在早上五點以前離開這裏，今晚必須在途中某地過夜。If we do not leave here by five in the morning, we shall have to stop somewhere on the way this evening.
4.假如有人跟他來，我們就不必到車站去接他。If anyone comes with him we do not have to go to the station to see him.
5.「我要是學了三年中文，我能夠容易地說中文嗎？」「當然，你能。」“If I learn Chinese for three years, shall I be able to speak it with ease?” “Of course, you will be able to.”
6.他聽了那件事時，嚇了一大跳。從那時起，他手腳都能夠動了。When he heard it he started up with fright. From that moment he was able to move hands and feet.
7.我上個月必須每三天去看一次牙醫。I had to go to the dentist’s every three days last month.
8.他後天可能不能來這裏，因為他每個星期四都很忙。He may not be able to come here the day after tomorrow, for he is busy every Thursday.
9.你無需重寫。You don't have to write it over again.
10.我們沒法得到好位子。We were unable to get good seats.

（10）

[The]

1.人被稱為萬物之靈，但是有些人不如貓和狗。Man is called the lord of creation, but there are some inferior to cats and dogs.
2.當我正要上電車時，有人輕拍了我的肩膀。那是我的朋友瑪麗。When I was about to get into the street car someone taped me on the shoulder. It was Mary, a friend of mine.
3.他握住我的手，不肯放開我。He, holding me by the hand, would not let me go.
4.亞馬遜河發源於安地斯山而注入大西洋，是世界第二長河流。The Amazon, which rises in the Andes and flows into the Atlantic Ocean, is the second longest river in the world.
5.在我國大部分工人是按月計酬而非按日。In out country most laborers are employed by the month, not by the day.
6.老虎是一種猛獸，和貓屬於同一種。The tiger is a fierce animal and it belongs to the same family as the cat.
7.他是現代的大音樂家，被稱為中國的華格納。He is a modern great musician, and he is called the Wagner of China.
8.菲律寶島位於台灣南方。它們以前屬於美國。The Philippine Islands (or The Philippines) lie to the south of Taiwan, Thy used to belong to the U.S.A.
9.砂糖和茶葉論克，而布料論公尺賣。Sugar and tea are sold by the gram, and cloth by the meter.
10.我們以小時計租了二部車。We hired two cars by the hour.

（11）

[Voice]

1.這隻可憐的狗被卡車輾過並且當場死了。The poor dog was run over by a truck and killed on the spot.
2.假如你問老師這種愚笨的問題，你將會被其他的學生取笑。If you ask your teacher such a stupid question, you will be laughed at by the other students.
3.他昨晚在公園散步時，突然遇到大雨而全身濕透了。 While he was taking a walk in the park last evening, he was caught in a shower and got wet to the skin.
4.他以按月支薪受雇，但是最初三個月沒領到薪水。He was employed by the month, but he was not given salary for the first three months.
5.那位乘客在火車上被偷了錶和錢包。 The passenger had his watch and purse stolen in the train.
6.他因為好心又熱忱，所以深受班上同學的好評。He is so kind and earnest that he was spoken well of by his school-fellows.
7.伯父給我這本書，但是它對我來說有點過於困難。I was given this book by my uncle, but it is a little too difficult for me.
8.我不能相信他的話，他是個說謊者。I cannot trust his words. He is a liar.
9.那個少年被警察認為是扒手。The lad was taken for a pickpocket by the policeman.
10.那個因誠實而被他們誇獎。The boy was praised by them for his honesty.

[Infinitive(1)]

1.飯後立刻做劇烈運動有害健康。It is bad for (the) health to take hard exercise soon after a meal.
2.在海裡游泳比在河裡容易，因為海水比淡水重。It is easier to swim in the sea than in the river, for salt water is heavier than fresh water.
3.在擁擠的電車或巴士上，讓座給老人和弱者是好的習慣。It is a good custom to give your seat to an old person or a weak one in a crowded car or bus.
4.在一個月左右學會一種外語，對任何人來說是不可能的。It is impossible for anyone to learn a foreign language in a month or so.
5.「這封信到達巴黎需要幾天？」「至少要四天。」“How long does it take for this letter to reach Paris? “ “At least it takes four days.”
6.從你家搭公車到戲院需要多久？走路需要花三十分鐘以上嗎？How long does it take to go to the theater from your house by bus? Does it take more than half an hour on foot?
7.這塊石頭重二百多公斤，需要四個人才抬得起。This stone weighs over 200 kilograms, and it takes four men to lift it up.
8.對任何人來說，隨身帶點錢是必要的，但是身懷鉅款是危險的。It is necessary for anybody to have some money with him, but it is dangerous to carry a large sum of money.
9.早起有益健康。To get up early is good for the health.
10.與其漫無目的地讀許多書，倒不如仔細地讀幾本書來得好。It is better to read a few books carefully than to read many at random.

（13）

[Infinitive(2)]

1.我今天有空，沒事可做。I am free today and l have nothing to do.
2.這裏可參觀的地方很多。如果你想全部看完，至少須停留一星期。There are many places to visit here. If you want to see them all you must stay at least a week.
3.「你在放學途中有沒有時間便到書店呢？」「不，我必須趕回家。」”Have you any time to step in the book-store no your way home form school?” “No. I must hurry home.”
4.我們為活而吃，並非為吃而活。We eat to live, and not live to eat.
5.「你打算買它嗎？」「不，我不打算買。」「我買不起。」Do you intend to buy it? No, I don’t. I cannot afford to buy it.
6.快十點啦！是睡覺的時間了，你如果煞夜便無法早起。It is nearly ten. It is time to go to bed. If you sit up late at night you will not be able to get up early in the morning.
7.你一到家，別忘了給他寫封謝涵。As soon as you get home, write him a letter of thanks without fail.
8.我想在飯前洗個手，請你借我肥皂和毛巾好嗎？Will you please lend me some soap and a towel, for I want to wash my hands before meal?
9.我今天要做的事很多。I have lots of things to do today.
10.他為了養家而辛苦工作。He works hard in order to support his family.

（14）

[Infinitive(3)]

1.這個箱子太小，不能容納所有這些書。”This box is too small to hold all these books.”
2.「這口井的水能喝嗎？」「不，不能喝。水很混濁。」“Is the water of this well good to drink?’’ “No, it is not. It is muddy.’’
3.你忘記這麼重要的事，真是個粗心的人。How careless you are to forget such an important matter!
4.習慣是第二天性。我們必須小心不要染上惡習。Habit is a second nature. We must be careful not to form a bad habit.
5.他太誠實不會說謊，太溫良不會出賣人。He is too honest a man to tell a lie, and he is too good-natured to betray others.
6.我前幾天把傘留在你家。很抱歉麻煩你，有機會時請你幫我送來。I left my umbrella at your house the other day. I am very sorry to trouble you, but will you please send it to me when you have an opportunity?
7.我去接他們時，他們已準備妥當隨時可以出發。When I called for them they were ready to start at moment.
8.兄弟們見了我將多麼高興啊！我有許多話想告訴他們。How pleased my brothers will be to see me! I have many things to tell them.
9.我將樂於陪伴你。I'll be glad to accompany you.
10.他答應不再偷懶。He promised not to be idle again.

（15）

[Infinitive(4)]
