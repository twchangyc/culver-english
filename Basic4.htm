第 四 個 基 本 句 型

請各位英文教師，善用以下的題例，先Copy下來，再依學生程度不同，予以增減內容，以期達到教學目標

主 語

述 語

ＮＰ

Ｖｉ~b(become, etc.)┼ NP (or) Adj

He

becomes a teacher.
註解1 Vi （不完全）不及物動詞。
註解2 Vi~b(Verbs of the become-type)，含有「become類的動詞」，後面再接著「名詞組」或「形容詞」，當做主語補語。
註解3 屬於become類的動詞，美式英語中有四個：become, remain, stay, continue.
註解4 屬於become類的動詞，英式英語除了以上四個之外，再加上seem, appear, look, turn等。
註解5 stay：（某狀態）繼續維持不變。If the weather stays fine, we will go to a picnic. // Stay young. 保持年輕。
註解6 有些「Vi （不完全）不及物動詞+as+補語」，as的後面接表示「身分」、「地位」、「資格」、「任務」的名詞。此名詞的功用在描述主詞，有主詞同位語的性質。as後面的單數形可數名詞通常不加不定冠詞。例：He acted as interpreter. // This noun functions as complement.

例句：

1.John became president.

2.He became famous.

3.His uncle remains a bachelor.

4.My uncle remains single.

5.The play continued an enormous success. 這齣戲繼續很成功地演下去。

進階例句：

1.The weather continued cold. 天氣持續寒冷。

2.She stays faithful to him for the rest of her life.

3.Mr. Green becomes the richest man in town.

4.Mr. Brown seems a very nice man.

5.They have stayed good friends for years.

6.He remained silent and said nothing.

7.Dick continues my best friend, despite our differences.

8.She remained a widow until death.

9.He became afraid and said nothing.

10.She will become a fine woman when she grows up.

英式英語例句：

1.The captain seemed a nice fellow.

2.The captain seemed very nice.

3.They appeared friends to all of us.

4.They appeared very much surprised.

5.He looks an honest man.

6.He looks honest.
