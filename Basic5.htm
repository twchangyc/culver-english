主 語

述 語

ＮＰ

 Ｖ~s (seem, etc ) ┼Ａｄｊ（形容詞）
The boy

seems very sorry.這男孩似乎很難過。

註解1

 V~s = 含有seem類的動詞(Verbs of the seem-type)

註解2

 屬於seem類的動詞有：seem, keep, look, get, grow, appear, smell, taste, turn,  sound, feel, ring, 等。這些動詞只能用「形容詞」來做主語補語。

註解3

 grow=get=become =turn 轉變,//grow old:漸老//grow angry:發怒

註解4

keep(vi):保持（某狀態），繼續 （某動作）。//1)Please keep quiet.2)The child keeps crying.3)He kept awake/alive.

　

例句：

1.It grows dark. 天黑了。

2.You have grown so tall. 你已長得這麼高了。

3.Bill looks happy.比爾看起來高興。

4.Helen seems friendly.海倫似乎很友善。

5.The milk tasted sour.這牛奶嚐起來酸。

6.The flowers smell sweet.這花聞起來香。

7.The story sounds interesting.這故事聽起來有趣。

8.They grew sleepy.他們變得想睡。

9.She looks beautiful.她看起來很美。

10.I feel happy.我感到快樂。

11.It feels soft.它摸起來很軟。

12.Sugar tastes sweet.糖嚐起來甜。

13.The music sounds nice.這音樂聽起來好聽。

14.The leaves turn red in autumn.在秋天樹葉變紅。

15.The dinner smells good.這頓晚餐聞起來不錯。

16.His dream came true.他的夢想果然實現了。

17.The well went dry.這口井乾了。

18.He seems very sorry.他似乎很難過。

19.You must keep silent in the class.你在教室裡一定要保持安靜。
