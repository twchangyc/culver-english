註解1 Vt~d(double-object verbs)雙賓動詞，這種動詞之後接兩個受詞，第一個是間接受詞(indirect object)，表「人」；第二個是直接受詞(direct object)，表「物」。
註解2 表「人」的間接受詞通常可以省去。例：Bill gave a book.//They are going to a nice present.//The student has asked a few questions.
註解3 但表「物」的直接受詞通常不能省去。例：Tom gave me.(×)//They are going to buy their daughter.(×)
註解4 「人」＋「物」可互換為→「物」＋介詞＋「人」；看詳解。
註解5 只能用「物」＋介詞＋「人」的動詞；看詳解。

「人」＋「物」（換為）→「物」＋介詞＋「人」

（一）間接受詞（人）為「接受者」，介詞用To

動詞包括：give, take, send, lend, rent, owe, pay, sell, tell, teach, bring, show, pass, throw, hand, offer, serve, read, write, sing, feed, drop, grant, deliver, do (good), etc.

例句：

1.Mary gave my brother a book.=Mary gave a book to my brother.

2.Bring me some water.=Bring some water to me.

3.Bring Tom the book.=Bring the book to Tom.（注意第二、三句，「人」「物」排列時，「物」不可用「代名詞」，Bring me it.(×)//Bring Tom it.(×)，如果「物」在「人」之前，則可用代名詞。）

4.He did me good.=He did good to me.他加惠於我。

5.My friend sent me the book.=My friend sent the book to me.我朋友送給我這本書。

6.Smoking will do you a lot of harm.=Smoking will do a lot of harm to you.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

（二）間接受詞（人）為「受惠者」，介詞用For

動詞包括：buy, make , cook, order, save, choose, get, paint, play, build, find, leave, call, pour, win, earn, catch, write, do (a favor), etc.

例句：

1.I bought my daughter a nice present.=I bought a nice present for my daughter.

2.I will do her a favor.=I will do a favor for her.

3.I will get you a ticket.=I will get a ticket for you.

4.Do you leave me any food?=Do you leave any food for me?

5.Her mother made her a pretty dress.=Her mother made a pretty dress for her.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

（三）間接受詞（人）為「來源」，介詞用Of

動詞包括：ask, expect, beg, demand, require, etc.

例句：

1.The student has asked the teacher a few questions .=The student has asked a few questions of the teacher.

2.May I ask you a question?=May I ask a question of you?

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

（四）間接受詞（人）為「對手」，介詞用With

動詞包括：play, run, etc.

例句：

1.They are going to play you a basketball game.=They are going to play a basketball game with you.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

只能用在：「物」＋介詞＋「人」的動詞，分二類：

（一）介詞用To： say, speak, talk, explain, report, describe, mention, remember, announce, propose, introduce, repeat, suggest, etc.

例句：

1.Say "hello" to him.(O)，不能用Say him "hello".(×)

2.John introduced Mary to his friend.(O)，不能用John introduced his friend Mary.(×)

（二）介詞用For： open, close, change, correct, keep, sign, answer, repeat, prepare, pronounce, translate, fix, etc.
