（天涯若比鄰）網際網路E-mail研習班英文範例

@以下列出在E-mail互動時，與使用英文的筆友，最常用到的例字和例句。

建議教師們，在學生尚未正式和對方E-mail時，先讓學生們學習有關基本英文書信用語，或可參考各種資料；學生一旦主動參與，則其成就指日可待。@

（一）重要的E-mail例字

【A】SEND（寄；送）

1.】我以E-mail郵寄信件。

I send a letter by E-mail.

2.】你送來了一個信息信給我。

You sent a message to me.

3.】我寄信給所有的朋友。

I sent letters to all my friends.

4.】他寄給我一封謝函。

He sent me a letter of appreciation.

5.】我父親每天送我上學。

My father sends me to school every day.

6.】如果你有空閒時間，請 E-mail給我。

If you have free time, please E-mail to me.

7.】信件被寄給我的所有朋友。

Letters were sent to all my friends.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

【B】Receive（收到）

1.）我已收到你的E-mail。

I have received your E-mail.

2.）昨天我收到你寄來的E-mail。

I received an E-mail from you yesterday.

3.）你會受到熱烈的歡迎。

You will receive a warm welcome.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

【C】Get

1.）我上星期收到你的E-mail。

I got your E-mail last week.

2.）我漸漸高了。

I am getting tall.

3.）我愈來愈感到有興趣。

I am getting more and more interesting.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

【D】Answer, Reply, Respond

1.）請給我回信。

Please answer a letter to me.

2.）他一句話也沒有回答我。

He didn't answer a word to me.=He didn't answer me a word.

3.）我問他如何做那件事，但他沒有回答。

I asked him how to do it, but he did not reply.

4.】我明天會回這封信。

I will reply to this letter.

5.】我不曉得要回答什麼。

I did not know what to reply.

6.】他回答我說，他已下定決心了。

He replied to me that his mind was made up.

7.】我還沒有得到你的答覆。

I have not heard your reply yet.

8.】他沒有回答。

He said nothing in reply.

9.】為了答覆那個問題，他要我參考最近的報紙。

In reply to the question, he referred me to a recent newspaper.

10.】他回答說他願意跟我做朋友。

He responded that he would like to make friend with me.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

【E】Hear

1.）你想聽一些有關台灣的訊息嗎?

Do you want to hear about the messages of Taiwan?

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

（二）剖析有問題的英文字和片語

1.大約在九點：「He will arrive at about nine o'clock.」是不對的，應只用at或about，不能兩字一齊用。

2.all-around(adj)「全盤的，多才多藝的」，應改用all-round較佳。

3.all of the people「所有的人」，應改為all the people。

4.at all times「永遠」，應改為always。

5.this time或the present time「此刻」，應改為now。

6.If you desire「如果你想要....」，應改為If you wish....或 If you want....。

On November 28 I received an E-mail from you. I am very happy to meet you on the Web. Your E-mail makes me feel wonderful. So I will keep on writing my E-mail and hope you become my best friend on the Web. If my English is not good enough, please give me a hand to modify it. Thank you so much.

參考應用《佳句》

1)我願將你介紹給我的朋友。

I'd like to introduce you to my friend.

2)我來介紹你給……

Let me introduce you to…….

3)我願你見見…….

I'd like you to meet…….

4)幸會。（高興遇見你）

Nice to meet you. = (Glad to meet you.)

5)我很高興與你相會。

I am glad to have met you.
