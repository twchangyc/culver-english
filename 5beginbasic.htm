一）         S+Vi

She laughed heartily.她笑得很開心。

[A] S + Vi (主語＋完全不及動詞)

範文：Clouds gathered. The sun dimmed. The sky darkened. The wind blew. Dogs ran and growled. Birds cried. Lightning flashed. Thunder sounded. The earth shook and trembled. Trees fell.  A hurricane had begun. (雲層聚集，陽光稀弱，天空轉暗，風吹著，狗又跑又叫，閃電，雷鳴，大地震動搖晃著，樹倒，颶已來到。)

[B] S + Vi + adverbial(副詞修飾語)

範文：Prop. Zhang worked hard today. He has been working at his office all day. Soon he will stop and go home. He will work until about 4:00. Then he will get in his car and drive home. He may drive fast. He wants to work in his garden for a while before dinner. Then he will dine quickly with his family. After dinner the family may read or listen to music. They may go bowling or go shopping at the nearby supermarket. They rarely go out socially during the week.(大意：張教授今天努力地工作，他整天工作，他很快就會停手，他要駕車回去，他會開得很快，他將安靜地用餐。)

[C] S + Vi + adverbial or prepositional phrase (介詞片語)

範文：Flight 007 is on the runway ready for takeoff. The passengers had waited in the airline terminal for one hour. The plane’s departure was announced over the loudspeaker ten minutes ago. They walked to the gate and climbed aboard the plane. Some passengers are traveling for pleasure, while others are going on business. Still others are traveling for family reasons. A few students are returning to their universities. The passengers will fly for about three hours. They will arrive at the airport in New York at 7:00 p.m.(大意：旅客已在航空站等候，擴音器廣播了起飛時間，他們走向機門，他們登上飛機，有些乘客為遊樂而旅行，有些人是為生意，學生們要回到他們的大學去，他們將飛行三個小時，他們將到達機場，他們將在下午七點著陸。)

（註）*表「狀態」的修飾語：I wrote carefully. (副詞); I wrote with care.(介詞片語);  I wrote if I had never written before.(副詞子句)

*表「場所」的修飾語：The house stands there. (副詞);  The house stands on the street corner. (介詞片語);  The house stands where the two roads meet. (副詞子句)

二）         S+Vi+SC

She is diligent. / She is a generous boss. /[除了Be動詞以外，此類動詞有：一、表變成‥:become, come, go, get, grow, turn, fall, run  二、表結果: prove, turn out, come out三、表仍然，繼續保持: remain, continue, keep, stand, rest  四、表似乎，感覺: seem, appear, look, sound, smell, taste, feel] She looks delicate.她看起來很優雅。I feel uncomfortable. / His dreams come true. / He seems very happy.

三）         S+Vt+O

I respect her. / She kept on saying that she was not guilty.她一直說她是無辜的。I like geography very much. / She didn’t know what to do. / Walls have ears.隔牆有耳 / The early bird catches the worm. [此類動詞，包含A:「不可分開的雙字動詞」：come across不期而遇，look into調查， search for尋找， account for說明，ask for要求，call for請求，stand for代表，do without無需，get over康復 ，例句：He called on me last night. / I will look after you. / He takes after his uncle. B:「可分開的雙字動詞」：例句：Turn on the radio, please. / Turn it off now. / Have you handed in your report? / I turned down his requests.]

四）         S+Vt+IO+DO

I wrote her a letter. / She left her husband a large fortune.她留給她老公一大筆財產。/ I bought her a birthday present.=I bought a birthday present for her. / I will do her a favor.=I will do a favor for her. / May I ask you a question?= May I ask a question of you? / Smoking will do you a lot of harm.= Smoking will do a lot of harm to you. / He told me how to operate the computer.

五）         S+Vt+O+OC

The letter made her happy. / She has recently had her appendix removed.她最近割了盲腸。/ Don’t keep her waiting long. / They elected him chairman. / I believe him honest. / I saw her enter the house. / Let sleeping dogs lie. / Tom likes his wife to dress beautifully.

1.         快告訴我比賽結果如何，別賣我關子。Tell me what happened at the end of the game. Don’t keep me in suspense.

2.         大部份被誘拐的女子，都是貧窮、未受教育，以及首次離家在外旅居者。Most of the women who are abducted are poor, uneducated, and traveling outside their home for the first time.

3.         很久之前，人們大都居住於室外。A long time ago, people lived mostly out-of-doors.

4.         電影將在九點開始。The movie will begin at 9 o’clock.

5.         在高度工業化之後，這個國家變得很繁榮。Being highly industrialized, this country has become very prosperous.

6.         這消息聽起來怪怪的，聽來像小說一樣。That news sounds odd (strange), it sounds like a fiction.

7.         那場表演結果很成功。That performance turned out very successful.

8.         她昨天上課時熟睡了。She fell fast asleep in class yesterday.

9.         這謠言證實是真的。The rumor proved true.

10.     在某些地區，來自水龍頭的水，有一股怪味。In some areas, water from the faucets has a very bad smell.

11.     在回家途中，我遇上一場陣雨。I was caught in a shower on my way home.

12.     急不暇擇。A drowning man will catch at a straw.

13.     她無法體會這個笑話。She didn’t catch on to the joke.

14.     如今許多人開豪華車，以炫耀財富。Nowadays, many people drive luxurious cars to show off their wealth.

15.     大約四百萬農民，目前正在找尋非農業的工作。Some four million peasants are currently looking for non-farming.

16.     他昨晚打電話給我。(以下不提供解答)

17.     當我走近時，他們停止交談。

18.     你去拿一把椅子給我好嗎？

19.     這個搶匪奪走了我的財物。

20.     有些人發現要與他交換意見是困難的。

21.     首先我要聲明清楚，我並不反對你的意見。

22.     她根本就沒有指望他幫忙。

23.     我認為此人對社會有所危害。

24.     中國字很難寫嗎？

25.     大多數的學生都不曉得如何使用圖書館。

26.     我的腳踏車上星期被偷了。

27.     天氣一天比一天熱起來。

28.     我連吃了五天葯，感冒還沒有好。

29.     我常常帶著我的老相機到郊外去。

30.     我喜歡拍鳥類和昆蟲的照片。

句型習作（選擇題）不提供解答

1.         The police are now_____ the case in order to find out the killer.(A)looking for (B)looking into (C)looking up (D)looking at

2.         Since he is in financial difficulty, he can _____ a new car. (A)look after (B)ask for (C)do without (D)stand for

3.         Mary’s boy friend called _____ her to go to the movies. (A)in (B)at (C)down (D)for

4.         The game was _____ off on account of the heavy rain. (A)taken (B)called (C)putting (D)to put

5.         To avoid being punished by his teacher, he _____ a story. (A)made up (B)carried out (C)picked out (D)cut down

6.         You have made a mistake in spelling _____ you _____ the letter “f”. (A)pointed out (B)turned out (C)put out (D)left out

7.         Taking exercise every day will _____ you much good. (A)make (B)do (C)push (D)hand

8.         John sang a song _____ Mary on her birthday. (A)for (B)to (C)on (D)of

9.         He denies nothing _____ his son. (A)with (B)on (C)to (D)for

10.     Save some of the pie _____ him. (A)of (B)to (C)at (D)for

11.     The movie reminded me _____ my first love. (A)to (B)of (C)on (D)for

12.     I heard Mary _____ a song a while ago. (A)sung (B)to sing (C)singing (D)sang

13.     I consider it _____ to master a language.(A)hardly (B)hard (C)with ease (D)easily

14.     I’m bored. Please _____ me alone.(A)leave (B)make (C)find (D)order

15.     My father doesn’t permit me _____ .(A)smoked (B)in smoking (C)smoking (D)to smoke

16.     Nobody has ever noticed anyone _____ her purse.(A)keeping (B)to give (C)made (D)take
