靜態動詞（stative verbs）& 中間動詞（middle verbs）

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

l         靜態動詞是屬於及物動詞，包括：have, resemble, fit, suit, mean, befall等。

l         靜態動詞不能改為被動。

l         靜態動詞不能出現於「祈使句」。

l         靜態動詞不用「情狀副詞」(manner adverb)來修飾。

例句：

1) John has a new bicycle. （不可改為：A new bicycle is had by John.）

2) The daughter resembles the mother. （不可改為：The mother is resembled by the daughter.）

3) His behavior does not fit ( =is not suitable for ) his important new position.

4) Rich food does not suit ( =agree with ) my stomach.

5) The dark clouds mean ( =are a sign of ) rain.

6) Some misfortune must have befallen ( =happened to ) them.

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

中間動詞( middle verbs )

有些語法學家，把以上的靜態動詞，與接度量衡名詞的動詞（如：weigh, cost, last, take, extend, stand, measure, number, total, amount等），合稱為「中間動詞」(middle verbs)，因為這些動詞，在語法功能上，介於及物動詞與不及物動詞之間。

例句：

1)     That car weighs well over two tons.（不能改為：Two tons is/are weighed by that car.）

2)     The book cost ( me ) fifty dollars. （不能改為：Fifty dollars is/are cost by the book.）

3)     The meeting will last a week.

4)     The debate took ( us ) two hours.

5)     The ranch extends two miles from east to west.

6)     My father stands six feet.

7)     The tree measures sixty feet in height.

8)     The students in that school number two thousand.

9)     Our expenses totaled ten thousand dollars.
