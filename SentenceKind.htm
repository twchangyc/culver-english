句子的種類（Kinds of Sentences）

句子依其結構可分類如下：

（一）Simple sentences（單句 ）

即，不含子句的句子，

例如：I want to buy it.

Mary and Betty are classmates.

Seeing me, he ran away.

　

（二）Compound sentences（合句=對等子句+對等子句）

例如：They went up, and we came down.

He is poor, but he is happy.

　

（三）Complex sentences（複句=主要子句＋從屬子句）

例如：I will let you know if he comes.

I know where he lives.

　

（四）Compound-complex sentences（複合句=對等子句＋對等子句＋從屬子句）

例如：It is true that he is old, but he is still strong.
