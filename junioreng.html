國中英文自我練習

【合併句子】

1.         He has a lot of money. + He wants more money. （用but 合併）

2.         He want to buy a new car. + He owns an old car. （用but 合併）

3.         Mr. Chen knew this. + Most people didn’t know this. （用連接詞 合併）

4.         He found a busy restaurant. + He sold his fruit there. （用where 合併）

5.         I like Mary. + He likes Mary. （用and so 合併）

6.         They got to the office building. + There were no parking spaces. （用連接詞 合併）

7.         He saw the two men. + The two men were standing in front of his restaurant. （用分詞 合併）

8.         They turned to leave. + A young man spoke to them. （用連接詞 合併）

9.         They finished eating. + The young man gave each of them an apple. （用連接詞 合併）

10.     He likes music. + I don’t like music. （用連接詞 合併）
