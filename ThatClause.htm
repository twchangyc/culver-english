That子句

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

例句：

I know that he has arrived.

That he passed the examination was a surprise.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

注意：

My brother insists on that you come along with me.(錯誤)→

My brother insists that you come along with me.(正確)（因為that之前的「介詞」一定要刪去）

類似句子：

Bob told me that she was sick.（that 之前的about刪去）

I am sure that I will succeed.（that 之前的of刪去）

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

◎可接「that子句」的動詞：

know, think, believe, tell someone, say ( to someone ), write ( to someone ), explain ( to someone ), report ( to someone ), reveal ( to someone ), hope, mean, deny, dream, consider, imagine, guess, suspect, assume, conclude, suppose, find ( out ), figure ( out ), learn, understand, see ( = understand ), hear ( =learn ), notice, feel, gather, ( not ) mind, trust, pretend, bet, remember, recall, forget, realize, discover, prove ( to someone ), doubt, fear, regret, prefer, agree, complain, decide, argue, claim, insist, inform someone, warn someone, remind someone, assure someone, notify someone, promise ( someone ), take care, make clear see to it等。

例句：I believe that he is honest.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

◎可接「that子句」的形容詞：

good, pleased, (un)satisfied, (un)happy, sorry, afraid, anxious, lucky, grateful, thankful, sure, certain, positive, confident, convinced, aware, conscious, careful, excited, upset, annoyed, disappointed, disturbed, worried, ashamed, surprised, amazed等。

例句：I am sorry that I am late.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

◎用於「It is + 形容詞 + that子句」的形容詞：

good, nice, fine, great, wonderful, marvelous, too bad, pitiful, shameful, right, wrong, sure, certain, clear, evident, apparent, obvious, true, natural, (un)likely, (im)possible, probable, lucky, (un)fortunate, strange, miraculous, sad, necessary, important, tragic, incredible, interesting, curious等。

例句：It is lucky that you met him then.

與上面形容詞意思相近的名詞（如a good thing, a shame, a pity, a miracle, no wonder等），也可以出現在這類句型。「It is + 形容詞 + that子句」

例句：It is a good thing that you have brought an umbrella with you.

        Isn’t it a pity that he failed again?

        It’s no wonder that she is popular with her friends.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

◎只能出現在「It  + that子句」的動詞：

seem ( to someone ), appear ( to someone ), look ( to someone ), ( so ) happen, ( so ) chance, follow, occur to someone, ( not ) matter, stand to reason, turn out, may be, must be, can ( not ) be, help a lot, hurt a lot等。

例句：It seems to me that he is very rich.

        It turned out that I was wrong.

        It may be that he missed the bus.

        It helped a lot that they lent us that extra money.

        Because he is good it does not follow that he is wise.

        It ( so ) happened that he was out of London at that time.
