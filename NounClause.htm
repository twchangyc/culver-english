名詞子句（Noun Clause）

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

◎子句定義：子句是句子的一部分，它含有「主語」和「述語」。換句話說，一個大句子中的小句子，叫做子句，又叫「句中句」。例如：

I don’t know when he will come.（我不知道他何時會來。）

I don’t know是「主要子句」，而when he will come是「名詞子句」，它是以when引導，附屬在「主要子句」，所以我們也稱這類「名詞子句」、「形容詞子句」、「副詞子句」為「附屬子句」。

重點一：附屬子句種類：「名詞子句」、「形容詞子句」、「副詞子句」

重點二：附屬子句不可以單獨存在，它必須要依附屬在「主要子句」身上，因為它不能單獨形成完整的意義。

重點三：在句子中，其功能相當「名詞」作用的子句，叫做「名詞子句」。

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

名詞子句的分類：（斜體字為名詞子句）

（一）當主語：

That the earth is round is true.

What he did was correct.

That she is happy is not true.

（二）當受詞：

Tom said that we were wrong.

I don’t know when he will come.

Do you know where he lives?

I cannot tell where she was born.

I agreed (to the opinion) that the meeting should be postponed.
She complained (of the fact) that he had been rude to her.
（agree和complain是不及物動詞，因其後省略「介+受」，名詞子句與其前的「受詞」，可視為「同位語」）

（三）當補語：

The truth was that he had not taken the money.

The question is who will bell the cat.

My first impression is that he is honest.

The trouble is that we are short of money.

（四）當同位語：

The news that Brazil was defeated surprised us.

This is the proof that he is honest.

I have no fear that she will not speak to me.

Don’t you know the saying that a rolling stone gathers no moss?

The report that he is dead must be false.

I am sorry (for the fact ) that I am late.
●（常接that子句的形容詞有：sorry, glad, sure, aware, surprised, delighted, disappointed等）

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

練習：請找出下例各句中，名詞子句在何處？

1.     That you would cheat me hurts me.

2.     No one knew exactly when he departed.

3.     That he comes from a poor family does not necessarily mean that he is dishonest.

4.     Before you buy that painting, you should make sure that it is genuine.

5.     Having heard him sing, I came to realize why he was popular with the pop fans.

6.     William dreamed that he was able to fly across the Atlantic in a balloon.

7.     That the accused was guilty was plain to us.

8.     What surprised us more was that he was able to speak French so well.

9.     I am very glad that I wasn’t there.

10.  Nobody disagreed with what you proposed.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------

●連接詞：wh~, how, that皆可當「名詞子句」的連接詞。(that, what, who, which, where, when, why, how)，例句：

1.     I don’t know who he is.

2.     I don’t know which he likes.

3.     I don’t know what his name is.

4.     I don’t know where he lives.

5.     I don’t know when he will come.

6.     I don’t know why he did not come.

7.     I don’t know how he did it.
