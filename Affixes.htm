Affixes接辭（Structure Of Word字的結構）

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

        許多英文單字，都是由一個最基本字（a base word = 字根root word）所衍生出來，也就是說，在字根的最前面，加上「字首Prefix」，或在字根的最後面，加上「字尾Suffix」，如此一來，一個單字立即可以變成，「二個以上」，與字根相關的單字了。

例如：    happy

                happy → unhappy （加上字首un）

                happy → happiness （加上字尾ness）

                happy → unhappiness （加上字首及字尾）

由上例得知，我們只要了解一個 happy 的意義，藉著「接辭」的功能，也就順便了解其他三個相關的字（unhappy, happiness, unhappiness）；換句話說，「字根」在英文中，是獨立存在的，而「接辭」則不能獨立存在；雖然它們本身具有意義，但它們一定要附加到「字根」上。

當我們熟悉了「接辭」的二種類型（字首與字尾）後，就必定能從有「接辭」的單字上，揣摩其相關含意；遇到有較長的單字時，我們也就可以猜出，它是由那一個「較小而具有意義的字根」，所演變出來。試試看，下例一些字的字根是什麼？

(1)worker                                (2)impossible                  (3)motherless

(4)disappear                        (5)freedom                      (6)hopeful

The Importance Of Vocabulary Growth（增長字彙的重要性）

        A good vocabulary and good reading go hand in hand. Unless you know the meaning of words, you will have difficulty in understanding what is read. And the more you read, the more words you will add to your vocabulary. Read the following statement:

        The misanthrope was apathetic to the sufferings of those around him.

Do you understand it? Unless you know the meanings of misanthrope and apathetic, you are not able to read the statement. In order to read, you must know the meanings of words and the way words are used in sentences.

        Acquiring word meanings is an important reading skill. Because of its importance, this skill is being presented in a text by itself.

~~~節錄「Gaining Word Power, p.1, by Dorothy Rubin」
