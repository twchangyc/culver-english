七十三學年度至九十學年度大學聯考英文科【中翻英】試題及解答

英文教師：張獻敏整理

七十三學年度

1.他點頭表示贊同。He indicated approval with a nod.
2.我深信寫日記的價值。I am convinced of the value of keeping a diary.
3.就交通安全而言，台北大部分駕駛員應該再受教育。As far as traffic safety is concerned, most drivers in Taipei should be reeducated.
4.假如當時受到鼓勵和幫助，他就不會失敗了。Given encouragement and help, he would not have failed.
5.強大的國防是和平與自由最確切的保證。A strong national defense is the most certain guarantee of peace and freedom.
6.運動對健康有益。Exercise is good for one's health.
7.我的腳踏車，上禮拜被偷了。My bicycle was stolen last Sunday.
8.大多數的學生，都不曉得如何使用圖書館。Most students do not know how to use a library.
9.我在今天的報紙上，看到一則很有趣的新聞。I read an interesting piece of news in today's newspaper.
10.要學好英文，必須下功夫。If you want to learn English well, you have to work hard.

七十四學年度

1.這一篇文章值得仔細閱讀。This article is worth reading carefully.
2.中國字很難寫嗎？Is it very hard to write Chinese characters?
3.那一位戴黃帽子的外國人是誰？Who is the foreigner that is wearing a yellow hat?
4.天氣一天比一天熱起來了。It is getting hotter day by day.
5.我不是告訴過你，我到車站的時候他們已經走了嗎？Haven't I told you that they had already gone when I got to the station?

七十五學年度

1.今天早晨有一位穿綠色制服的人，把信件送到我們的社區來。Every morning a man in a green uniform delivers mail to our community.
2.無論是晴天或下雨，天天都可以看到他。Rain or shine, we can see him every day.
3.如果有掛號信要送給我們，他就會在門口按一下鈴。Whenever he has registered letters for us, he will ring the doorbell.
4.他認識我們附近的每一個人，而我們每一個人也都認識他。He knows everyone in our neighborhood, and everyone of us also knows him.
5.有時候我在上學的途中遇到他，他就以微笑跟我打招呼。Sometimes I meet him on my way to school, and he greets me with a smile.

七十六學年度

1.很多學生都不太注意讀書的方法，以至於浪費許多寶貴的時間。Many students pay so little attention to reading skills that they waste a lot of precious time.
2.他們讀英文讀了好幾年，還是看不懂相當容易的文章。They have studied English for many  years, but they still cannot understand articles which are rather easy.
3.其實，只要方法正確，英文的閱讀能力並不難培養。In fact, as long as the method is correct, English reading ability is not difficult to develop.
4.在老師還沒有講解課文之前，一定要自己先讀兩三遍。Before your teacher explains the text to you, you must go over it two or three times.
5.不要讀太難的文章，也不要遇到生字就立刻查字典。Don't read articles which are too difficult for you and don't look up a new word in a dictionary the moment you see it, either.

七十七學年度

1.英語是一種重要的語言，所以很多人都在學習。English is an important language, so many people are learning it.
2.許多中國學生認為學習說英語很難。Many Chinese students think that it is very hard to learn to speak English.
3.但是只要經常練習，任何困難都可以克服。But if you keep practicing speaking English, you can overcome any difficulty.
4.會背課本上的對話，並不表示會說英語。Memorizing the dialogues in the text does not mean that you are able to speak English.
5.最重要的是，要能用英語說自己想說的話。The most important thing is that you should be able to say whatever you want to say in English.

七十八學年度

1.讀小學的時候，我身體很虛弱，並且常感冒。When I was in elementary school, I was very weak and often caught colds.
2.自從上了國中以後，我就儘量找機會運動。Ever since I entered junior high school, I have been trying hard to find opportunities for exercise.
3.雖然功課一直很繁重，我還是每天慢跑半小時。Although my schoolwork has been heavy, I have managed to jog for half an hour every day.
4.六年繼續不斷的鍛鍊使我不但更加強壯，而且也長得更高。Six years of constant training has made me  not only stronger but also taller.
5.現在大家都說他們無法相信我從前又矮又瘦。Now everyone says that they cannot believe that I was short and thin before.

七十九學年度

1.昨天早上我出門的時候，天氣相當暖和。It was quite warm when I left home yesterday morning.
2.所以我沒有穿毛衣，只穿了一件襯衫。Therefore, I didn't wear a sweater but only a shirt.
3.但是到了下午，溫度卻急速下降了。But the temperature dropped sharply in the afternoon.
4.雖然晚上比往常早回家，我還是覺得有點不舒服。Though I got back home earlier than usual in the evening, I still felt slightly under the weather.
5.今天我不但打噴嚏而且頭痛，我想我最好馬上去看醫生。Not only am I sneezing today, but I also have a headache. I think I had better see a doctor right away.

八十學年度

1.我生長在鄉下的一個小村落。I grew up in a small village in the country.
2.那時，我家附近有一條清澈的小溪。At that time, there was a clear brook near the house where I lived.
3.我夏天常到那裡游泳、釣魚。I used to go swimming and fishing there in the summer.
4.現在溪水髒得連魚都不能活。Now the water in the brook has become so filthy that fish cannot live in it.
5.我不知道甚麼時候才能再見到童年的美景。I have no idea when I'll be able to see that beautiful view I remember from my childhood.

八十四學年度

1.台灣的生活最近十年變了很多。Life in Taiwan has changed quite a lot over the past ten years.
2.年輕的一代有許多新的生活方式。The younger generation has many new ways of living.
3.和他們父母親那一輩相當不同。Young people are quite different from their parents.
4.舉個例，很多年輕人喜歡吃美式速食，喝加糖飲料。For example, many young people like to eat American fast food and consume sweet drinks.
5.而老一輩的大多寧可吃中國菜，喝中國茶。However, the older generation would rather eat Chinese food and drink tea.

八十五學年度

1.每次放假，我都會到台灣某個地方去旅行。Whenever we have a vacation, I'll travel somewhere in Taiwan.
2.今年春假，我和一些同學搭巴士到墾丁國家公園。This spring vacation, I went with some of my classmates by bus to Kenting National Park.
3.我們在那兒待了三天，很欣賞那裡的山林和海岸。We stayed there for three days, appreciating the mountain forests and sea.
4.這學期還沒結束，可是我們就在計畫夏天的旅行了。This semester is not over yet, but we are planning for our summer vacation travels.
5.我們大概會到台灣東部去，因為那兒風景很美。We'll probably go to the east of Taiwan for the scenery there is very beautiful.

八十六學年度

2.那時，人們會活得更長久。Then, people will live longer.
3.那的確是個好消息。That is nice indeed.
4.但是會有一個問題：But there exists a problem:
5.更多的老人必須由更少的年輕人奉養。There will be more senior citizens to be supported by fewer young people.

八十八學年度

1.年輕人和老年人的睡眠習慣很不相同。Young people's sleeping habit is very different from that of old people.
2.許多年輕人往往很晚還不睡，第二天早上就不能早起。Many young people often stay up so late that they can't get up early the next morning.
3.老年人通常晚上沒事做，所以很早上床。Old people usually have nothing to do, so they go to bed early.
4.這兩種不同的睡眠習慣，哪一種比較有益健康呢？Of these two different habits, which is better for health?
5.其實，只要睡飽，早睡晚睡都沒關係。In fact, as long as you get enough sleep, it doesn't matter whether you go to bed early or not.

5.聽起來你今天很倒楣。Sounds like you really had a bad day. ( It sounds like today wasn't your day. ) ( You seem to have had a really bad day. )
