信件結尾的好用詞句

(1)Thank you for your help. (or) Thank you for your help, Mr. Chang.

(2)Thank you, and I look forward to hearing from you soon.

(3)Thank you, Mary, for considering our request. We look forward to hearing from you soon.

(4)Thank you for giving me the opportunity to serve you. [or] Thank you again for sending me the form.

(5)Thank you, Ms. Chang, and I hope to hear a response from you soon.(可將soon改為within a day or two)

(6)If you have any questions, please let me know.

(7)If you need anything else, let me know.

(8)If you have any questions, please contact me by phone.(可將by phone改為immediately)

(9)If there is anything I can do to help, please let me know.

(10)If you need more information, please write or call me.

(11)If I can do anything else to help, please let me know.

(12)Please email me your confirmation of our notice as soon as you receive this letter.

(13)Please call or write me if I can help you in any other way.

(14)Call or write if there is anything else I can do to help.

(15)Please send me one through internet mail.

(16)In the meantime, if you have any questions, please call or write me.

(17)In any case, I wish you luck with your training center. I am really sorry to miss this opportunity.

(18)Talk to you again soon.

(19)In the meantime, I hope you and your classmates find this article useful and entertaining.

(20)I know you like war movies. Well, here's a site that's entirely devoted to war fans. I hope you enjoy it.

(21)I appreciate your help. If you have any questions, please feel free to contact me at any time.

(22)I look forward to hearing from you soon.

(23)I hope this information is helpful. Please contact the course clerk.

(24)I hope you'll understand that the assignment is extremely urgent.

(25)I apologize for the oversight on my part and thank you for helping me take care of it.

(26)I look forward to your reply.

(27)I hope to see you soon.

(28)I hope to continue contacting with you in the future.

(29)I have no idea why there are so many flaws (mistakes) in my composition. Please explain and revise it. Thanks a lot.

(30)Please let me know what I should do.
