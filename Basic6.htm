述 語

ＮＰ

 Ｖｉ+ （Ａｄｖ~ｍ）（不及物動詞+表示狀態的副詞 ）

It

rained (hard) yesterday.

註解1

不及物動詞後面常常接「表示狀態的副詞」(Adv~m, adverbial of manner)，如happily, slowly, soundly等來修飾。

註解2

表示狀態的副詞大多數都是由形容詞加「ly」而成的。試比較：The boy is careful.→ The boy studies carefully.// They were very happy.→ They lived very happily.

註解3

有些不及物動詞後面可以跟著另一類副詞：如up, down, on, off, in, out, over, by, away等。例：They didn't show up. 這類up的副詞，也可稱為「介副詞」(preposition-adverbs)。因為其中有很多都可以當介詞用。看詳例。

例句：

1.Money talks.

2.They walked (slowly).

3.Jack is working (happily).

4.The woman lived very sadly. 這女人很悲傷地生活。

5.The baby is sleeping (soundly).

6.The boy studies carefully. 這個孩子很細心地讀書。

7.The dog ran off.

8.They didn't show up.

9.Sam walked away quickly.

10.He got up very early last Monday.

11.He works hard in order to succeed.

12.He waited there until I came.

13.The package weighs ten pounds.

14.The meeting lasted ( for ) two hours.

15.My books sell well.

16.I usually sleep eight hours a day.

不及物動詞+介副詞

1.He came in [ out, back, home ].

2.She'll soon be here.

3.The bird has flown up [ down, away ].

4.This knife does not cut well.

5.This book reads better than that one.

不及物動詞+介詞＋(代)名詞

1.He came into [ out of ] the room.

2.They sat on the sofa [ in the chairs, beside us ].

3.This house belongs to my father.

4.Everybody mocked at the boy.

5.The committee consists of ten members.

6.You may rely on [ upon ] my early arrival.

7.He is out in the garden.

8.He burst out [ forth ] into explanations.
