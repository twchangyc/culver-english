同源賓語

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

²        有些英語的不及物動詞，可以接上語形相同，或語意相似的名詞做賓語，語法上叫「同源賓語 cognate object」。這些賓語，通常都帶有形容詞等修飾語，以加重述語的份量。例句：

1)     He slept a sound sleep ( =slept soundly).（不可改為：A sound sleep was slept by him.）

2)     She laughed a merry laugh( =laughed merrily). （不可改為：A hearty laugh was laughed by her.）

3)     I dreamed a beautiful dream last night.

4)     He died a natural death.

5)     The king lived a life of an exile.

6)     They are running a race.

7)     They fought a brave battle.

8)     We have struck a blow for freedom.

9)     She smiled ( a smile of )thanks ( =expressed her thanks by smiling).

10)  He nodded ( a node of ) his approval ( =nodded approvingly ).

●偶爾，含有同源賓語的句子，也可以改為被動句，例如：(1) His last fight was fought in that home. (2) Life cannot be lived without much charity.

²        最高級形容詞後面的同源賓語，常省去。

例句：

1)     He breathed his last ( breath ).

2)     They shouted their loudest ( shout ).

3)     He was trying his best to marry her.
