古今名句

張獻敏整理 2017 Nov 17

1.         Never miss a chance to keep your mouth shut. ~~~[ A Day No Pigs Would Die ]有機會閉嘴，千萬不要放過。

2.         Never assume the obvious is true.~~~[William Safire, Sleeper Spy]決不可假定顯然即為確然。

3.         Even more exasperating than the guy who thinks he knows it all is the one who really does.~~~[Al Bernstein, National Enquirer]比起自以為是的萬事通，真正的萬事通叫人吃不消。

4.         You can discover more about a person in an hour of play than in a year of conversation.~~~[柏拉圖]觀查人品，看他玩耍一小時猶勝跟他交談一年。

5.         When you can’t have what you want, it’s time to start wanting what you have. ~~~[Kathleen A. Sutton]如果得不到你所愛的，就愛你所得到的。

6.         Children are the most expensive form of entertainment. ~~~[Mihaela losof]兒女是最昂貴的娛樂形式。

7.         Often we have no time for our friends but all the time in the world for our enemies. ~~~[Leon Uris, Redemption]人往往沒空和朋友把晤，卻有花不完的時間和敵人周旋。

8.         Temptations, unlike opportunities, will always give you second chances. ~~~[O.A. Battista]誘惑和時機不同，總是會給你第二次機會。

9.         It has been my philosophy of life that difficulties vanish when faced boldly. ~~~[Isaac Asimov, foundation]這一直是我的人生哲學，勇於任事，困難自消。

10.     A leader does not impose a decision, he molds one. ~~~[曼德拉]

11.     Words are plentiful, but deeds are precious. ~~~[華里沙]

12.     Better to be occasionally cheated than perpetually suspicious. ~~~[B.C. Forbes]偶然被騙總好過永遠疑人。

13.     Where there is great love, there are always miracles. ~~~[Willa Cather, Death Comes for the Archbishop]有偉大的愛，就永遠有奇蹟。

14.     Sports do not build character. They reveal it. ~~~[Heywood Hale Broun]運動不能培養品格；運動顯露品格。

15.     Thinking is the hardest work there is, which is probably the reason so few engage in it. ~~~[Henry Ford]思考一事至難，或因此世人甚少用腦。

16.     More things grow in the garden than the gardener sows. ~~~[西班牙諺語]

17.     One measure of leadership is the caliber of people who choose to follow you. ~~~[Dennis A. Peer in National Enquirer]從追隨者之優劣，可以看出領導才能一斑。

18.     Forgiveness is a gift of high value. Yet its cost is nothing. ~~~[Betty Smith, A tree Grows in Brooklyn]寬宥是極矜貴的禮物，卻不費分文。

19.     A great many people mistake opinions for thoughts. ~~~[Herbert V. Prochnow]許多人誤以意見為思想。

20.     Tranquility is like quicksilver. The harder you grab for it, the less likely you will grasp it. ~~~[Bern Williams]安靜猶如水銀，越是用力去抓，越是得不到。

21.     When we seek to discover the best in others, we somehow bring out the best in ourselves. ~~~[William Arthur Ward]我們設法從別人身上發掘優點時，無意間已表露了自已的優點。

22.     History, although sometimes made up of the few acts of the great, is more often shaped by the many acts of the small. ~~~[The Wall Street Journal]歷史有時是由大人物一二舉措所寫成，但更是由小人物的普遍行為所構成。

23.     Life shrinks or expands in proportion to one’s courage. ~~~[Anais Nin, The Diary of Anais Nin] 人生起落以意志勇怯而定。

24.     Good manners will open doors that the best education cannot. ~~~[Clarence Thomas] 良好教養能打開最佳學歷所打不開的門。

25.     There are no speed limits on the road to excellence.~~~[David W. Johnson]邁向卓越之路並無速度限制。

26.     Careers, like rockets, don’t always take off on schedule. The sky is to keep working on the engines. ~~~[Gary Sinise, quoted in USA weekend]事業就像火箭並非總是依時升空，關鍵是始終保持動力。

27.     Making long-term decisions requires short-term difficulties. ~~~[Larry Demarest, quoted in Time]要作長遠的決定勢必面對眼前的困阻。

28.     There are two situations that make interesting stories; when an extraordinary person is plunged into the commonplace, and when an ordinary person gets involved in extraordinary events. ~~~[Sister Helen Prejean, Dead Man Walking]兩種情況可以構成有趣的故事；非常之人置身平常情境，或平常之人捲入非常事態。

29.     Management by objectives works if you first think through your objectives. Ninety percent of the time you haven’t. ~~~[Peter F. Drucker]以目標為本的管理工作，只要事先把目標想清楚了，便行得通，你十次有九次沒想清楚。

30.     Man only likes to count his troubles, but he does not count his joys.~~~[Fyodor Dostoyevsky, Notes From Underground]  世人往往計憂不計喜。

31.     If a window of opportunity appears, don’t pull down the shade. ~~~[Tom Peters, The Pursuit of Wow!]機會之窗一現，切莫拉下窗簾。

32.     Prayer is less about changing the world than it is about changing ourselves. ~~~[David J. Wolpe, Teaching Your Children About God]祈禱不是為了改變世界，而是為了改變我們自已。

33.     A truly great book should be read in youth, again in maturity and once more in old age, as a fine building should be seen by morning light, at noon and by moonlight. ~~~[Robertson Davies, The Enthusiasms of Robertson Davies]真正的好書應在少年時一讀，成年時重讀，老年再讀；就像美麗的建築，應在晨曦，正午及月下觀賞。

34.     Big doesn’t necessarily mean better. Sunflowers aren’t better than violets. ~~~[Edna Ferber, Giant]大些未必好些，向日癸並不比紫羅蘭好。

35.     My father always told me, “Find a job you love and you’ll never have to work a day in your life.” ~~~[Jim Fox]父親總告訴我說：「找一份你喜歡的工作，這輩子就一天都不用熬了。」

36.     The most important outcome of education is to help students become independent of formal education. ~~~[Technology Review]教育最重要的效益是要讓學生自立於正統教育之外。

37.     Having children makes you no more a parent than having a piano makes you a pianist. ~~~[Lessons at the Halfway Point]生兒育女不足為父為母，就像有了鋼琴未必成為鋼琴家。

38.     It is easier to love humanity as a whole than to love one’s neighbor. ~~~[Eric Hoffer]愛世人易，愛鄰人難。

39.     Patriotism is not a short and frenzied outburst of emotion but the tranquil and steady dedication of a lifetime. ~~~[史蒂文生]愛國斷非一時迸發之激情，而是一生不渝之忠心。

40.     The healthiest competition occurs when average people win by putting in above-average effort. ~~~[Colin Powell, My American Journey]尋常之人出非常之力而取勝，此之謂最健康之競爭。

41.     The trouble with talking too fast is mou may say something you haven’t thought of yet. ~~~[Creators Syndicate]快嘴快舌之弊在於所說的話可能未經深思。

42.     Ability is what you’re capable of doing. Motivation determines what you do. Attitude determines how well you do it. ~~~[Lou Holtz]表現取決於能力，行為取決於動機，成就高下則取決於態度。

43.     Without music, life is a journey through a desert. ~~~[Beach Music]沒有音樂，人生猶如沙漠之旅。

44.     Revenge has no more quenching effect on emotions than salt water has on thirst. ~~~[Newsweek]報復不能平息憤懣，就像海水不能止渴。

45.     Time makes heroes but dissolves celebrities. ~~~[Parade]歲月造就英雄，卻使名人褪色。

46.     The worst disgrace one can do to the truth is to know it and yet to abandon it. ~~~[Bossuet]最玷辱真理的，莫過於明知是真理卻予漠視。

47.     Humor is not a trick, not jokes. Humor is a presence in the world—like grace—and shines on everybody. ~~~[Garrison Keillor]幽默不是捉弄人，不是說笑話，幽默是世間華采，有如萬千優雅，照亮每一個人。

48.     A good example is like a bell that calls many to church. ~~~[丹麥諺語]典範如同招引眾生上教堂的鐘聲。

49.     In every community, there is work to be done. In every nation, there are wounds to heal. In every heart, there is the power to do it. ~~~[Marianne Williamson, A Return to Love]每個社會都有缺失待補，每個國家都有創痛待愈，每個人內心都有補缺愈創的力量。

50.     Lord, how the day passes! It’s like a life—so quickly when we don’t watch it, and so slowly if we do. ~~~[John Steinbeck, East of Eden]天啊，時光消逝如斯，恰似人生—我們一不留神它轉眼即逝，留神的時候它又過得那樣緩慢。

51.     Children have more need of models than of critics. ~~~[Things Your Dad Always Told You But You Don’t Want To Hear]兒童需要身教多於言教。

52.     To love and be loved is to feel the sun from both sides. ~~~[How to Live With Another Person]有施有受的愛，猶如兩面都感受得到陽光。

53.     There are no limits on our future if we don’t put limits on our people. ~~~[Jack Kemp]不對人民設限，便是不為我們的前途設限。

54.     When the mouth stumbles, it is worse than the foot. ~~~[西非諺語]失言之害，甚於失足。

55.     A different language is a different vision of life. ~~~[Federico Fellini, quoted in New York Times]出身有別，人生觀互異。

56.     It is one thing to learn about the past; it is another to wallow in it. ~~~[Kenneth Auchincloss, Newweek]鑑古知今與沉溺於過去是兩回事。

57.     Life is the biggest bargain. We get it for nothing. ~~~[猶太諺語]降生人世是最划算的，我們不費分毫。

66.     We always hear about the haves and the have-nots. Why don’t we hear about the doers and the do-nots? ~~~[Creators Syndicate]我們總是聽到大家說誰有什麼誰沒有什麼，為什麼聽不到大家談誰盡了力誰沒盡力？

134.  There is a time in the life of every problem when it is big enough to see, yet small enough to solve. ~~~[Mike Leavitt]任何問題出現，都有一段時間大得無法視而不見，而又小得可以解決。

135.  A novel is like a violin bow, and the body of the violin, which resonates, is the soul of the reader. ~~~[Stendhal]小說有如小提琴的弓，產生共鳴的琴身則是讀者心靈。

136.  Discovery consists of seeing what everybody has seen and thinking what nobody has thought. ~~~[Albert Szent-Gyorgyi]發現之義包含見人所曾見，卻能思人所未思。

137.  Friendship is certainly the finest balm for the pangs of disappointed love. ~~~[Jane Austen, Northanger Abbey]友情肯定是醫治失戀之痛的良藥。

138.  It’s a funny thing about life; if you refuse to accept anything but the best, you very often get it. ~~~[毛姆]人生十分奇妙；假如你非最好的不要，十之八九能如願以償。

139.  You can measure a man by the opposition it takes to discourage him. ~~~[Life Lessons]衡量一個人，端看需要多大阻力才能使他挫退。

140.  The hero reveals the possibilities of human nature; the celebrity reveals the possibilities of the media. ~~~[Parade]英雄揭示人性的潛力; 名人揭示傳媒的潛力。

141.  Anyone who says you can’t see a thought simply doesn’t know art. ~~~[Wynetka Ann Reynolds]說思想是看不見的，其實不懂藝術。

142.  Marriage resembles a pair of shears, so joined that they cannot be separated; often moving in opposite directions, yet always punishing anyone who comes between them. ~~~[Marriage]婚姻有如剪刀，雙刃併結難分；雖常互相朝反向移動，誰要是夾介其間，定會遭殃。

143.  An intellectual is a man who takes more words than necessary to tell more than he knows. ~~~[艾森豪]知識分子也者，夸夸其談，喜以超乎所需之詞論超乎所知之事。

144.  When the search for truth is confused with political advocacy, the pursuit of knowledge is reduced to the quest for power. ~~~[In a Dark Wood]尋覓真理與政治主張混為一談，知識之追求則淪為爭權奪勢了。

145.  Great nations write their autobiographies in three manuscripts --- the book of their deeds, the book of their words and the book of their art. ~~~[John Ruskin]偉大的國家以三個稿本撰寫國史 --- 功績卷、語錄卷和藝術卷。

146.  The difference between reality and fiction? Fiction has to make sense. ~~~[Tom Clancy, “Larry King Live”, CNN]真實與虛構的區別何在？虛構必須合情合理。

147.  The young do not know enough to be prudent, and therefore they attempt the impossible --- and achieve it, generation after generation. ~~~[賽珍珠]年輕人少不更事，不知天高地厚，因此勇赴不可能之事，竟有所成，代代如此。

148.  A hero is someone we can admire without apology. ~~~[New York Times]英雄者，吾人衷心景仰而無需費辭辯解者是也。

149.  Well-timed silence is the most commanding expression. ~~~[Mark Helprin in The Wall Street Journal]恰到好處的緘默是最有力的表達方式。

150.  If you are too careful, you are so occupied in being careful that you are sure to stumble over something. ~~~[Gertrude Stein]過於小心翼翼，難免只顧及小慎微，結果一定會給什麼絆倒。

151.  There is no power on earth that can neutralize the influence of a high, simple and useful life. ~~~[Booker T. Washington]高遠質樸而有益世道之人生，其風範所及，無力可摧。

152.  Say what you have to say, not what you ought. ~~~[梭羅]說你一定要說的，而非應該要說的話。

153.  The greatest gift you can give another is the purity of your attention. ~~~[Richard Moss, M.D.]真誠關注是你能給人的最佳贈物。

154.  The game of life is a lot like football. You have to tackle your problems, block your fears, and score your points when you get the opportunity. ~~~[Don’t Sit Under the Grits Tree With Anyone Else But Me]人生近似踢美式足球，你必須擒伏困難，堵截恐懼，一有機會就達陣取分。
