The book

is on the table.（這本書在桌上。）

註解1

 Adv~p (adverbial of place)表示場所的副詞。

註解2

表示場所的副詞可能是單獨的副詞，如here, there, inside, outside, upstairs, downstairs, up, down, in, out。

註解3

表示場所的副詞，也可能是「副詞片語」，如 over there, in the house, at the door, behind the table, by the river,...etc。

註解4

 Be動詞可以翻譯成「在」。

例句：

1.John is here.約翰在這裡。

2.We are in the classroom.我們在教室裡。

3.The next town is three miles away.下個城鎮在三哩外。

4.The girl in a red dress is in back of the desk.

注意1：如果這個句型的主語為無定的名詞的話，就要變成由There帶頭的句子。例如：My book is on the table. =（有定主詞：「我的書在桌子上。」）//There is a book on the table. =（無定主詞：「桌子上有一本書。」，由There帶頭的句子，又可稱為「存在句」）

1.There is a ________ on the desk. 書桌上有一支筆。

2.There are three boys _______________. 外面有三個男孩。

3.There _________ a lot of people _________________________ yesterday.

昨天公園裡有很多人。

4.There ________ some letters ___________ you ______ the table.

桌子上有一些你的信。

◎第三個基本句型與「存在句」之互換：

NP + Be + Adv~p

There + Be + NP + Adv~p

例句：

1.A book is here. → There is a book here.

2.There boys are outside. → There are three boys outside.

3.Two dogs are in the garden. → There are two dogs in the garden.

4.Some money has been in the purse. → There has been some money in the purse.

互換練習：（把下列句子改為由There帶頭的句子）

1.A car is outside.

2.Only a footpath was here last year.

3.A sign is over the door.

4.A gas-station is three miles away.

5.Some letters for you are on the table.

6.No one was in the room.

7.A lot of people were in the park yesterday.

8.An old friend of yours was at the concert last night.

9.A check will be in the mail next month.

10.Several students might have been in the library.

注意2：與有定主語一起出現的there或here是副詞，要唸重音（強調最前的場所副詞）。含有這樣的there或here的句子，不能變成否定句或問句。

1.There's the accident.（有定，There's 要唸重音）//There's an accident.（無定，accident要唸重音）

2.Here's the party.（有定，Here's 要唸重音）

3.There isn't the accident.(×) 要改為→There isn't an accident. (O)

4.Is there the accident?(×) 要改為→Is there an accident? (O)
